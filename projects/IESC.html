PROJECT // TRAFFIC LIGHT CONTROLLER SYSTEM

PROBLEM STATEMENT

In urban areas, traffic congestion at intersections leads to increased travel times, higher vehicle emissions, and reduced efficiency of the transportation network. Traditional traffic light control systems are static and unable to adapt to varying traffic patterns throughout the day — some lights stay green too long with little traffic, or red too long during peak hours. The lack of real-time data analysis and adaptive response exacerbates traffic management challenges in densely populated cities where patterns are complex and dynamic.

TELEMETRY // IESC JUDGING PANEL

OUR SOLUTION

An AI-Driven Traffic Light Controller System combining edge computing and artificial intelligence to dynamically manage traffic flow at intersections based on real-time data:

  • Real-time Traffic Data Collection: a network of high-resolution cameras at each intersection captures and analyzes vehicle counts and queue lengths during red-light phases.
  • Intelligent Control Algorithm: an AI model processes collected data to predict traffic patterns and dynamically adjust light timings — minimizing wait times and maximizing road capacity.
  • Edge Computing Infrastructure: data is processed locally at the intersection for immediate decision-making without cloud dependency or network latency.
  • Customizable Adaptive Models: AI models adapt to specific traffic conditions and intersection geometries, continuously improving predictions and control strategies.
  • Data Privacy & Security: no personal data is collected or stored during monitoring; all data transmission is secured.

EXPECTED BENEFITS

  • Reduced Congestion: dynamic light timing shortens travel times and uses road networks more efficiently.
  • Lower Emissions: decreased idling reduces emissions, improving air quality and sustainability.
  • Enhanced Safety: optimized traffic flow reduces the likelihood of accidents.
  • Scalable & Maintainable: designed for easy scaling to additional intersections with ongoing performance optimization and upgrade plans.
