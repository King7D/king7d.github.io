HEART DISEASE PREDICTOR

ML Risk Assessment on the Cleveland Dataset

MISSION BRIEF

Heart disease remains a leading cause of mortality globally, with early detection playing a crucial role in managing and mitigating its impact. Using machine-learning techniques, this project enhances predictive models that assess heart-disease likelihood from multiple predictors — helping healthcare professionals diagnose swiftly and accurately for prompt, effective intervention.

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Develop a predictive model on the Cleveland Heart Disease dataset that estimates the likelihood of heart disease from medical attributes and lifestyle factors — focusing on predicting severity using variables such as chest pain type, resting blood pressure, and thalassemia across age groups and genders.

DATASET DESCRIPTION

The Cleveland Heart Disease dataset includes individuals evaluated at the Cleveland Clinic Foundation. Key attributes used in predictive modeling:

  • Chest Pain Type: type of chest pain experienced by the patient.
  • Resting Blood Pressure: the resting blood pressure level of an individual.
  • Thalassemia: a blood disorder affecting hemoglobin.
  • Age & Sex: demographic attributes of the individual.
  • Additional attributes: cholesterol levels, fasting blood sugar, resting electrocardiographic results, and more.

USAGE

Input the required medical parameters as specified by the model's input requirements. The model outputs a probability score indicating the likelihood of heart-disease presence.
